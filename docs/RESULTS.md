## Apparent winding

No source in the pilot sample passed every strict CW/CCW reliability criterion.

Of the **16 objects** that reached the robust winding stage:

- **7** had CW as the diagnostic candidate;
- **9** had CCW as the diagnostic candidate;
- **0** were accepted as strict CW or CCW.

Therefore this pilot provides **no basis for claiming a population-level winding asymmetry**.

## Why strict winding was not accepted

The current robust stage rejects results when one or more conditions fail, including weak signed margin, instability to reasonable parameter changes, weak explained spiral structure, or poor separation between spiral and straight/bar-like templates.

This is a feature of the methodology: a weak preference is retained as a diagnostic candidate while the final label remains `UNCERTAIN`.

## Next validation target

Before interpreting morphology or handedness fractions astrophysically, the pipeline should be evaluated on a labeled reference set with known morphology and visually secure spiral winding.
